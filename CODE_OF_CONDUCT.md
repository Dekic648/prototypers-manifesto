# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Be kind, be constructive, assume good faith. Prototypes are how we think out
loud — the same generosity applies to reviewing each other's work.

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds this community:

- Critiquing the prototype, not the person who made it.
- Giving and gracefully accepting feedback — including feedback that a principle
  you proposed does not belong.
- Assuming a contributor's rough draft is a question, not a claim.
- Focusing on what is best for the manifesto and the people who read it.

Behaviour that will not be tolerated:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will take
appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour they deem
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. They have the right to remove,
edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are
not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the maintainer, [@Dekic648](https://github.com/Dekic648), by opening
a [private security advisory][advisory] on this repository — it is visible only
to maintainers — or through any private channel listed on that GitHub profile.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers
are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any
incident.

[advisory]: https://github.com/Dekic648/prototypers-manifesto/security/advisories/new

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the repository, its
issues and pull requests — and also applies when an individual is officially
representing the project in public spaces.
